# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors with respect regardless of background, identity, or experience. Discuss technical ideas constructively, credit others, and welcome questions. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, and disclosure of private information are unacceptable.

Report concerns privately to repository maintainers through GitHub's private reporting options when available. Do not post personal information in public issues. Maintainers may remove harmful content, warn participants, or restrict participation, proportionate to the incident. Maintainers are also accountable to these rules.
